### 🎙️ Recruiter Opening Script – Professional Version

> Recruiter Introduction:

Hi [Candidate’s Name], it’s great to meet you! My name is [Your Name], and I work as a recruiter with **Nex4 Inc.** and **300 Million Legacy LLC**. We’re specialized in the entertainment and AI staffing domain and have a strong presence in the U.S. tech market. Our clients include a wide range of top-tier companies — including several **Fortune 500 software engineering firms**.

We’ve recently been engaged by several of these clients to help them **build high-performing Data Engineering teams**. That’s where you come in — we're currently looking for skilled engineers who are ready to take on exciting new roles in impactful environments.

> Purpose of the Interview:

Today’s meeting is the **first step in the hiring process**. It’s an initial screening where I’ll ask a few brief questions to better understand your **professional experience**, **current availability**, and **career goals**. If your profile aligns with what our clients are looking for, I’ll submit your information directly to the hiring manager.

If the manager gives us a thumbs up, we’ll move you forward to the next stage, which may include **technical interviews**, **panel discussions**, and potentially a **final offer discussion**.

Does that sound good to you?

> Candidate Questions:

Great! Let’s get started.

1. **Can you tell me a little bit about yourself and the most recent project you've been working on?**
    
2. **What’s your current employment status?** Are you actively working, between roles, or open to new opportunities?
    
3. **If you receive an offer after the final interview, what would your notice period be before you can start?** Ideally, our clients are looking for **immediate joiners**, but we understand a **one-week notice** may occasionally be needed.
    
4. **Are you available to work full-time (9 AM to 5 PM, Central Time)?** Our clients operate primarily in the U.S. Central Time Zone, so availability during those hours is essential.
    
5. Regarding compensation, the planned salary range for these roles is **$2,500 to $4,000 USD per month**, depending on experience. For exceptional candidates, the offer may go up to **$5,000 USD**.
    
    **Does that align with your expectations for your next role?**
    

> Wrap-Up:

Thanks for sharing that! Based on your responses, I’ll be compiling a report that includes your resume and a summary of today’s conversation for our internal team. If everything checks out, we’ll reach out soon with the next steps.
