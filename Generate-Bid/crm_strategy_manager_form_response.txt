   - Transaction history

   - Segmentation strategies for targeted campaigns
   - Retention intervention programs
   - LTV optimization initiatives
   - Churn prevention tactics

5. **Measurement & Iteration**: Regular measurement cycles to track progress:
   - Weekly/monthly performance reviews
   - A/B testing capabilities
   - Continuous model refinement
   - Iterative strategy improvements
